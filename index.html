How it works

Each plate is art and also a claim about what an algorithm or an integrator does. Four habits keep the claim honest.

Seeded

A plate draws its randomness only from a generator seeded by the URL. With the same parameters, a shared link redraws the same picture; Reseed draws a new one.

Measured

Every readout is computed from the running simulation's own state: comparisons and writes as performed, frontier sizes, energies and their drift. No constant poses as a measurement.

Tested

Algorithm cores and integrators are unit-tested against independent references: node:crypto for SHA-256, brute-force sums for Barnes–Hut, Ising and Voronoi, known sequences for automata and Collatz. Read the tests

Gated

Before Pages deploys, a real-browser gate mounts every plate and checks that it draws and reports, runs axe-core for WCAG 2.2 AA, and allows no console error or overflow, in light and dark, on phone and desktop. Read the gate
